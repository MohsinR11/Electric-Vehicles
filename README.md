# ⚡ **Electric Vehicle Data Analysis Dashboard** 🚗

---

Welcome to the **Electric Vehicle Data Analysis Dashboard!** 🔋  
This **Tableau project** delivers a **deep dive** into EV data — highlighting **vehicle distribution**, **electric range**, **CAFV incentives eligibility**, and more.  
It analyzes **150,413 vehicles** across makes, models, states, and years, with interactive visuals for powerful insights. 📊

---

## 🔑 **Key Metrics**

- 🚗 **Total Vehicles:** 150,413  
- 📏 **Average Electric Range:** 67.83 miles  
- 🔌 **Total BEV Vehicles:** 116,745 _(77.62% of total)_  
- 🔋 **Total PHEV Vehicles:** 33,668 _(22.38% of total)_

---

## 🛠️ **Dashboard Highlights**

This dashboard is designed for **exploring EV trends** with ease. Use filters to customize your analysis and uncover market insights! 🔍

---

## 📊 **Visualizations**

### 📅 **1️⃣ Vehicles by Model Year**

- **Histogram** showing EV count by **model year (2010–2024)**.  
- 📈 **Trend:** Peak in 2023 with **37.1K vehicles**, yearly average of **10.7K**.

---

### 🗺️ **2️⃣ Vehicles by State**

- **US map** visualizing EV distribution across states.  
- 🌎 **Highlight:** **Washington** dominates with **150,082 vehicles**; e.g., **North Carolina** has just **35**.

---

### 🏭 **3️⃣ Top 10 Vehicles by Make**

- **Bar chart** ranking EV manufacturers by count.  
- 🏆 **Leader:** **Tesla** tops the list with **69,929 vehicles (46.47%)**, next is **Chevrolet** with **12,024**.

---

### ✅ **4️⃣ CAFV Eligibility**

- **Pie chart** of Clean Alternative Fuel Vehicle (CAFV) incentive status.  
- ⚖️ **Breakdown:**  
  - 69.7% **Eligible**  
  - 17.8% **Not Eligible**  
  - 12.5% **Unknown**

---

### 🚘 **5️⃣ Vehicles by Model**

- **Table** listing top models with make, EV type, vehicle count, % of total.  
- 🔝 **Top Model:** **Tesla Model 3** — **27,708 vehicles (18.42%)**, followed by **Tesla Model Y** with **20,901**.

## 🧩 **Filters**

Refine your view with:

- 🚘 **Model:** All or specific _(e.g., Model 3, Leaf, etc.)_  
- 🔌 **EV Type:** All, **BEV**, **PHEV**  
- 🌍 **State:** All or selected states  
- ✅ **CAFV Eligibility:** All, Eligible, Not Eligible, Unknown

---

## 🚀 **How to Use**

1️⃣ **Open the Dashboard:**  
   Use **Tableau Desktop** or **Tableau Public**.

2️⃣ **Apply Filters:**  
   Filter by Model, EV Type, State, or CAFV Eligibility. 🎛️

3️⃣ **Explore:**  
   Hover for details, click charts to drill down. 🖱️

---

## ⚙️ **Requirements**

- **Tool:** Tableau Desktop or Tableau Public  
- **Dataset should include:**  
  - Vehicle Details _(make, model, year, EV type)_  
  - Geographic Info _(state)_  
  - Performance _(electric range)_  
  - Incentive Data _(CAFV eligibility)_
